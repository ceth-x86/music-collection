# MTV Unplugged

Started at the end of the 80s with Bon Jovi, the MTV unplugged series quickly became the place to be for trendy groups... in unplugged style! Nearly all the big names had their turn: Eagles, Bob Dylan, Björk, Alanis Morissette, R.E.M., The Corrs, Neil Young, Oasis, Jimmy Page & Robert Plant, Mariah Carey, Florence + The Machine... But two concerts in particular stand out: Eric Clapton and Nirvana who respectively sold 7 and 5 million copies of their "MTV Unplugged" performances.
