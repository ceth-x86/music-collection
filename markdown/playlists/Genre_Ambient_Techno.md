# Genre: Ambient Techno

Ambient Techno is a subgenre that fuses the driving, hypnotic rhythmic foundations of techno with the atmospheric, expansive textures of ambient music. It prioritizes mood, sound design, and immersive "headspace" over traditional dance-floor energy, typically featuring subtle, recurring synth pads, deep melodic layers, and a steady, unobtrusive beat.
