# Genre: Modern Prog Rock

Modern Progressive Rock is a genre that blends the intricate musicianship, long-form compositions, and conceptual depth of 1970s prog with contemporary production techniques, modern heavy metal elements, and atmospheric textures. It is defined by its refusal to adhere to traditional song structures, prioritizing technical virtuosity, complex time signatures, and immersive, often cinematic, soundscapes.
