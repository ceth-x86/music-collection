# Dark Folk, Neoclassical Folk

Neoclassical Folk is a dark folk subgenre characterized by orchestral instrumentation, classical composition structures, and chamber-folk aesthetics. It blends neofolk’s acoustic, earthy darkness with neoclassical elements like string ensembles, piano, and choral vocals. This niche sits under the Dark Folk umbrella (alongside sibling styles like Apocalyptic Folk, Martial Folk, Pagan Folk, etc.)
